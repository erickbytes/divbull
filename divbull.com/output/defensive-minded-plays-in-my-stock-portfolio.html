Defensive Minded Plays in My Stock Portfolio

Gold (SGOL)

I decidend recently to start buying gold. The main motivation came from reading about "The Weird Portfolio", which I highly recommend reading. Reading this blog post inspired me to think The Weird portfolio allocates 25% to gold! My allocation is now about 1% to SGOL. I am thinking I might raise it as high as 5% over time. I made the decision to buy SGOL for extra geographic diversification. When I buy the ETF, I have my physical gold stored in a vault in Switzerland, not the United States.

Real Estate Investment Trusts aka REITs (O)

Buying REITs means you are sacrificing potentially higher returns in exchange for stability and less severe drawdowns compared to tech stocks. I allocated more recently to Realty Income. It pays a monthly dividend. This holding is exclusivly held in my tax advantages accounts, so I don't pay taxes on the dividend yield.

Utilities (XLU)

Similar to REITS, you don't buy utilities expecting maximum return versus more growth-y stocks. I allocated a small position to utilities because they could be a long term winner in the AI buildout. Buying utilities means owning the electric power grid.

Short Term Treasury Bonds aka "T-Bills" (SGOV)

I use SGOV to earn 3.88% on my cash holdings. Cash is a position. It is necessary to keep to maintain liquidity in your life. It also enables you to be timely in seizing opportunities in the stock market.

Value Stocks (VBR, IJS)

Value stocks are outperforming growth in 2026. allocated to VBR and IJS, value stock funds. Value stocks are doing better than the Mag 7 this year. It won't be like that every year.

Dividend Stocks (TM, KO)

I have some smaller dividend stocks holdings like Coca-Cola and Toyota. Coca-cola trades at an swollen multiple, but the business is unflappable and continues to execute its playbook.

Consumer Staples (COST)

Costco trades at a shy-high valuation. It's an incredible business. It is in the staples sector, which tends to perform better in tougher economic times. Rain or shine, people are going to Costco and will love it.

S&P 500 (SWPPX and VFIAX)

More often than not, I would have been better off just buying the market versus stockpicking. Although the S&P index is capable of steep drawdowns, it is certainly more defensive in nature than picking the winners of the future. However, don't we wouls be wise to include some international exposure in the portfolio.
