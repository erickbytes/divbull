Hedging My Tesla Shares With SpaceX Shares

I am a long term Tesla investor. I've held the stock for over 7 years. I've added to my position and taken profits at different times.

It recently occurred to me that I might hedge my Tesla stake against a SpaceX buyout. I reasoned that if the two merge, divesting some of Tesla stake to play the other side could be a hedge against a transaction that seems feasible to occur. I did not invest in SpaceX solely because of this logic, but it was a benefit I considered when assessing SpaceX. For example, I like owning SpaceX's Starlink business also.

I began accumulating SpaceX on July 1st. Since then I've added more shares. I've brought my SpaceX stake to 14% vs. 86% for my Tesla to SpaceX ratio.

My SpaceX position was funded from underperforming stocks and cash position since July. I also sold one share of Tesla and directly reinvested into SpaceX. I now feel some form of insurance against the looming Tesla-SpaceX buyout deal. Sentiment seems to point to this happening sometime in the future.
