Title: Market Observations With 75% of 2026 Complete 
Date: 2026-10-07 4:52 
Category: Essay


The stock market has remained resilient in 2026. Despite headlines raising investor fear, the S&P 500 has hovered steadily near all-time highs.
With 75% of 2026 in the books, it is time to reflect on themes and trends I've noticed as the year has played out.


**REITs and High Rates Don't Mix**

I remarked in February how strong REITs were performing. That momentum has evaporated. Higher interest rates have been the trend. 
That is bad for the REIT business model.

**Magnificent 7 Regains Its Strength**

After stumbling earlier this year, most of the Magnificent 7 rebounded in Q2 or Q3. Tesla has been a noticeable laggard. Microsoft had been as well until its recent hot streak in July, August and September.


**META Stock Bounces Back With Muse**

Meta sentiment was in the gutter because their capex was swelling with time. Additionally, investors wondered, "how the outrageous capex would translate into profits?" Then Meta dropped Muse, an AI agent that investors now believe answers the question, at least partially. The stock was down for most 2026. Then in September, it Muse revived it.


**Robotaxis Are Inevitable**

Waymo, Tesla and Zoox, oh my! Las Vegas, Austin, Miami and more are the proving grounds for the tech I only thought I'd see in sci-fi movies, not in my lifetime. Expansion continues for all of these companies. Google's Waymo seems to have achieved the most scale so far, thanks to their first mover advantage. The only thing that stands in the way of these trailblazers are resistance from people and their slow government regulations.


**SpaceX Launches Successful IPO**

SpaceX appears to have bucked the IPO tankathon trend that seems to happen to so many stocks making their public debut. 
So far it has evaded that fate and made the transition to public markets smoothly. The business is humminh and impressively 
hitting objectives so far.


**Korean Tech Heats Up**
SK Hynix and Samsung became mainstream stocks for US investors in 2026. I've never heard more chatter online about Korean stocks.
SK Hynix is up 164% YTD. Samsung is up 124%. EWY, the MSCI KOREA 25/50 INDEX is up 89%. South Korea has some of the hottest stocks 
on the planet right now.  


**Coca-Cola Stock Claims Cola Superiority**
Coca-Cola stock went up this year by 24% as of Oct. 7th. Pepsi is -13% in the same time period. 


**Oil and War Fatigue Slams Markets With Minor Effect**

The war with Iran continues on. The USA is now 223 days into its conflict with Iran.
It's been unexpected how it hasn't really seemed to change much, aside from adding a bit more volatility in stock prices.
Oil and the strait of Hormuz were all you heard about months ago. They are still important, but haven't seemed to move the needle after 7 months.
