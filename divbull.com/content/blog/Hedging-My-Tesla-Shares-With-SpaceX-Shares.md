Title: Hedging My Tesla Shares With SpaceX Shares 
Date: 2026-10-09 17:15 
Category: Essay


I am a long term Tesla investor. I've held the stock for over 7 years. I've added to my position and taken profits at different times.

It recently occurred to me that I might hedge my Tesla stake against a SpaceX buyout. I reasoned that if the two merge, divesting some of Tesla stake 
to play the other side could be a hedge against a transaction that seems feasible to occur.

I began accumulating SpaceX on July 1st. Since then I've added more shares. I've brought my SpaceX stake to 14% vs. 86% for my Tesla to SpaceX ratio.

![Tesla vs. SpaceX stock allocation pie chart]({static}/images/SPCX-vs-TSLA.png)

My SpaceX position was funded from underperforming stocks and cash positon since July. I now feel some form of insurance against the looming Tesla-SpaceX buyout deal. Sentiment seems to point to this happening sometime in the future.
