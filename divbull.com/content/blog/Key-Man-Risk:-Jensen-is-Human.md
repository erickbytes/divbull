Title: Key Man Risk: Jensen is Human 
Date: 2026-11-10 9:54 
Category: Essay

Invessting will teach you new lessons over time. A new risk was revealed to me this week: key man risk.
The biggest companies tend to have "superstar" CEOs. This is a huge asset for any company. However,
it is an equally large liability.

Jensen Huang made headlines Monday for his affair with an alleged Chinese spy. There are potentially all kinds of conflicts that
arise from this relationship. Given the USA's lock and key over sending Nvidia's chips to China, then opening up sales again, and now 
potentially more restrictions coming, this is more or less a disaster for Nvidia. It is also a huge distraction. Additionally, they will now need to comply with government investigations. Additionally, the man who was so influential in Nvidia's rise is now negated by his own decision-making.

IT's also a lesson in diversification for me. Nvidia was my highest conviction stock before this weekend. Now, I need to assess this murky situation 
and decide what, if any action to take. 

Heading into this past weekend Nvidia held a 14% of my stock portfolio. I was planning to sell shares around $250, but didn't quite get there before this recent drawdown. Now I hold Nvidia still as the top position at 12%.

My gut says the fundamentals have only improved. This feels like a short term black swan event for Nvidia. So I am reluctant to reduce position now.

I will continue to monitor the news as it breaks and its implications. The big question is how much did Jensen give to the spy. Was her relationship with him used to procure chips? If it was, how big of a problem is that? The situation is heavy. As shareholders, we are paying the costs for Jensen's 
infidelity. But just how big will those costs be? Again, my instinct says this will all blow over.

I think back to the crowdstrike fiasco when they blue screened a bunch of computers around the world. Two years later, the stock is up over 200% from that black swan. Granted, this situation is more pressing for Nvidia because their key man took a risk. I will be monitoring closely and ready to reduce position size if this goes the wrong way for Nvidia. However, as it stands I believe they will get through this.
